About BFT Trade Lab

BFT Trade Lab is an independent trading education and practice platform based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. It helps traders learn price action, practise on real historical market data with demo money, test an idea on unseen data, and keep a journal to find the habits that cost money.

What we do not do

  • We do not give buy or sell tips, signals or calls.
  • We do not manage money or take deposits for trading.
  • We do not promise profits. Trading carries a high risk of loss.

What we offer

  • Journal: log trades, see your leaks, weekly reports.
  • Testing Lab: replay history, backtest and forward-test with demo money.
  • Classroom and Tools: teach and calculate.
  • Academy: structured courses, notes and indicators (paid).
