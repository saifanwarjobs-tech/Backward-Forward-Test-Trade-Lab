🎓 Welcome to the Classroom

A blank board for teaching price action. Three steps:

  1. Pick a lesson: tap 📈 Breakout or ⚠️ Breakout Failure. Or draw your own price path with 🕯 Candle Path: the candles follow it.
  2. Explain: press Space to pause, ⏭ to step one candle, 🔴 Laser to point, ✏️ Pen to write.
  3. Record: hide the toolbar with ☰, press ● Record, talk, then Stop & Download.

Made a mistake? ↶ Undo, ⌫ Eraser, or 🗑 Reset board to start fresh.
