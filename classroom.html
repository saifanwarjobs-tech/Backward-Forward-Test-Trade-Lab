🎓 Welcome to the Classroom

A blank board for teaching price action. Three steps:

  1. Start: draw your own price path with 🕯 Candle Path (candles follow it), or open 📚 Lessons, search and pick from 100+ ready lessons (breakouts, traps, patterns, candlesticks).
  2. Explain: press Space to pause, ⏭ to step one candle, 🔴 Laser to point, ✏️ Pen to write.
  3. Record: hide the toolbar with ☰, press ● Record, talk, then Stop & Download.

Made a mistake? ↶ Undo, ⌫ Eraser, or 🗑 Reset to start fresh.

🗑 Reset the board?

Every drawing, candle and lesson will be erased. You can still press Undo right after to bring it back.
