Questions

Who can join?

Anyone with a confirmed email. You do not need to be a customer. It is free.

When do I get paid?

A sale first shows as Pending for 10 days, because customers have a 7-day refund window. After that it becomes Approved and is paid to your UPI once a month. Balances under ₹500 carry forward.

What if the customer asks for a refund?

No commission is earned on a refunded sale. If a refund happens after we already paid you, it is deducted from your next payout.

How long does my link stay active for a visitor?

30 days. If someone opens your link and buys within 30 days on the same browser, the sale is yours. If they open another partner's link later, that partner gets it.

Can I use my own link to buy?

No. Purchases by you, or by accounts that belong to you, do not earn commission.

Do I earn on the people my referrals bring in?

No. There is only one level. You earn on the sales you refer yourself.

Programme rules

  1. Commission. 30% of the amount the customer pays at checkout, on every purchase made through your link within 30 days of their click. The rate shown in your dashboard is the one that applies to you.
  2. Approval. A sale is pending for 10 days (the refund window is 7 days), then approved. Refunded sales earn nothing. A refund after payout is deducted from your next payout.
  3. Payout. By UPI, once a month, for approved balances of ₹500 or more. Smaller balances carry forward.
  4. No self-referral. You cannot earn on purchases by you, your family accounts, or accounts you control. We cancel such commission and may close the account.
  5. Honest promotion. BFT Trade Lab is education and practice software. Do not promise profit, returns, tips, calls or signals. Do not claim to be BFT. No spam, no fake accounts, and no paid ads on our brand name.
  6. One level. You earn only on sales you refer yourself, not on other partners.
  7. Tax. You are responsible for tax on your earnings. We may ask for details such as PAN, and may deduct tax at source where the law requires it.
  8. Changes. We may change, pause or end the programme or a partner account. Commission already approved is still paid, unless it came from a breach of these rules.
