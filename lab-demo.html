Testing Lab ko step-by-step kaise use karein

Pehli baar aaye ho to ye order follow karo.

1
Rule likho (kagaz par)Entry kab, SL kahan, target kitna. Jab tak rule likha nahi, test mat shuru karo.
2
Replay mein 20-30 trades loMarket aur timeframe chuno. Next se candle aage badhao, BUY / SELL se trade lo, Levels se SL aur target lagao.
3
Report dekhoWin rate akela kuch nahi batata. Profit factor, expectancy aur drawdown dekho (Report, History).
4
Rule mein ek hi cheez badloZyada badloge to apne aap ko dhokha doge. Ek change, phir dobara test.
5
Forward test se proof loRules lock karo (Lock rules & start), phir unseen data par chalao. Yahi asli imtihaan hai.
