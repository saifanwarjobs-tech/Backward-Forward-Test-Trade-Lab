Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

What we collect

Journal, Lab and Tools data (trades, notes, settings, checklists) stays on your own device in your browser storage by default, and we do not receive it. If you clear browser data it is gone, so use Export. If you choose Cloud backup in the Journal, your trades are copied to our database (Supabase) and linked to your account. You can delete the cloud copy at any time from the same screen.

Academy account data: your email, your phone number if you share it for payment or support, your plan, amount paid, payment reference and expiry date. Messages you send us on WhatsApp or email.

Why we use it

To give you access to paid content, confirm payments, answer support questions and send service messages. We do not sell your personal data.

Who helps us run the site

Your Academy login and course data are stored with our database provider (Supabase). Payments are handled by Razorpay; we do not see or store your card or bank details. Some scripts load from public content networks. We do not use advertising trackers.

Website analytics

To see how many people visit and which pages help them, we run our own simple analytics (stored in our Supabase database, not shared with ad networks). For each page view we record: the page, the site you came from, device type, operating system, browser, screen size, language, an approximate location (country, state and city), how long the page was open, how far it was scrolled, and which buttons or links were used. A random ID is kept in your browser storage to tell visits apart. We do not record your IP address, name, email, phone number, anything you type, or any trade, note or setting in the Journal, Lab or Classroom. To work out your approximate city, your browser asks a public IP-location service (ipwho.is), which sees your IP address as any website you visit would. You can stop analytics by opening this site once with ?notrack=1 added to the address (for example index.html?notrack=1) or by blocking site storage.

How long we keep it

Academy records are kept while your account is active and for the period needed for accounts, tax and dispute records. You can ask us to delete your account data sooner, except where we must keep a record by law.
