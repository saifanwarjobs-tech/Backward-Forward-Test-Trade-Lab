How it works

Three steps, about five minutes to your first honest result.

1️⃣

Practise

Pick any past day. Press Next to reveal candles and trade with demo money. Mark zones as you go.

2️⃣

Lock your rules

Write your exact rules and lock them. No edits, no rewinding, no peeking.

3️⃣

Forward test

Trade the reserved unseen period. The lab compares Backtest and Forward and tells you if your edge held up.
