FAQ

Is the Lab free?

Yes. The Testing Lab and Classroom open in your browser with no sign-up. Premium Journal and Academy are premium tools.

Where is my journal data saved?

On your device, in this browser. Use Export for a backup. Cloud sync is planned.

Do you give tips or signals?

No. Everything here is education and practice, with historical data and demo money.

Which market data is used?

BANKNIFTY 5-minute history and Gold (XAUUSD) M15 / M30 / H1 history. The data comes from third-party sources, is for education only, and may contain gaps or errors. You can also load your own data with the Data button in the Lab.
