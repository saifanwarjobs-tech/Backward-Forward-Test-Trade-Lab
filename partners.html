What traders do on BFT Trade Lab

Testing LabReplay real history (BANKNIFTY, Gold, Bitcoin). A backtest on one part of the data, then a locked forward test on data the trader has not seen. The rules are fixed before the forward test starts.
Risk reportWin rate, profit factor, expectancy, drawdown, payoff ratio, losing streak and recovery factor, as a shareable report.
JournalTraders log trades and tag their own mistakes, so they see which habits cost them money.
AcademyPrice-action lessons in Hindi and English, with practice on demo money.

Why this matters to a broker

  • Our traders learn position sizing, stop-loss and drawdown before the first live trade, which is where many beginners fail.
  • They build the habit of testing a plan first, so we aim to send you serious, patient clients rather than one-trade accounts.
  • Hindi-first content reaches traders in tier-2 and tier-3 cities, based in Jaipur, Rajasthan.
