Refund, cancellation and delivery

Last updated 19 October 2026.

What you are buying

A digital product: online access to the Academy (courses, PDF notes, indicators). Nothing is shipped. Prices are in INR.

Delivery

Access starts after your payment is confirmed, usually within a few minutes (automatically) and in any case within 24 hours. Sign in with the same email you used at checkout. If you did not get access, contact us and we will fix it.

7-day refund

If the Academy is not right for you, ask for a refund within 7 days of your payment date, as long as you have completed less than 20% of the lessons. This stops full courses being finished and then refunded.

How to ask

Message us at saif.anwar.jobs@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 63505 66010 from the contact details you used to buy, with your registered email. Tell us briefly why; it helps us improve.

When you get the money

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days. Your bank may take extra time to show it.

Cancellation

There is no subscription or auto-renewal. You pay once, so there is nothing to cancel later.

Double payment or failed access

If you were charged twice, or paid but did not get access, contact us at any time. We will fix it or refund the extra amount.

Not refundable

Accounts that were shared or misused, and requests made after the refund window.
